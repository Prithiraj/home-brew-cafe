A neighborhood room

Come for the coffee. Stay for the pace.

Skip to My Brew is less about rushing through a transaction and more about finding a corner that feels easy to return to—a booth, a book, a game, and something well made in your hand.

The menu moves between familiar espresso drinks, sweet-foam signatures, tea, pastries, and seasonal ideas. The room keeps the welcome simple: free Wi-Fi, comfortable seating, and enough personality to notice something new each time.

For the morning
Classic espresso, cappuccino, cortado, and tea.
For the afternoon
Seasonal flavors, pastries, a laptop, or a long conversation.
For the table
Books, board games, and small whimsical details around the room.

A thoughtful little detail

Cold brew, without the fade.

Coffee ice cubes keep the drink from watering down as the afternoon unfolds. It is a small choice, but it says something about the place: playful, practical, and attentive all at once.
